RISK MANAGEMENT POLICY
Acme Financial Services

1. Purpose
This policy establishes how Acme identifies, assesses, and responds to
cybersecurity risks.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all information systems and business processes at
Acme that involve handling of company or customer data.

3. Risk Identification
The IT department identifies potential risks to information systems through
periodic reviews.

4. Risk Assessment
Identified risks are informally discussed by the IT team and prioritized
based on team judgment.

5. Risk Response
For significant risks, the IT Manager decides on an appropriate response,
which may include mitigation, acceptance, or transfer.

6. Vendor Risk
New software vendors are reviewed by IT before procurement approval.

7. Reporting
Risk-related updates are shared with department heads as needed.

8. Review
This policy will be revisited periodically.
