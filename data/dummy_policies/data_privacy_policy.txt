DATA PRIVACY AND SECURITY POLICY

1. Purpose
This policy establishes rules for how the company handles personal and
sensitive data.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees who handle customer data.

3. Data Collection
The company will only collect data that is necessary for business purposes.
Employees should avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.

4. Data Storage
Customer data is stored on company servers. Access is restricted to
authorized personnel.

5. Data Sharing
Data will not be shared with third parties without appropriate justification.

6. Employee Responsibilities
All employees must handle data responsibly and report any suspected data
breaches to their manager.

7. Policy Review
This policy will be reviewed periodically.
