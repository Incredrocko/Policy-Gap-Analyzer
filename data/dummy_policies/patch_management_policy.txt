PATCH MANAGEMENT POLICY
Acme Financial Services

1. Purpose
This policy defines how Acme applies software patches and updates to its
systems to reduce security vulnerabilities.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all servers and workstations owned by Acme.

3. Patch Sources
Patches are obtained from official vendor websites and update services.

4. Patch Testing
Critical patches are tested by IT staff before being applied to production
systems where practical.

5. Patch Deployment
The IT team applies patches on a monthly schedule. Emergency patches for
actively exploited vulnerabilities are applied as soon as possible.

6. Roles
The IT team is responsible for identifying and applying patches.

7. Exceptions
Systems that cannot be patched due to compatibility issues will be noted
by the IT team.

8. Recordkeeping
The IT team keeps a spreadsheet of patches applied each month.
