INFORMATION SECURITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (ISMS) POLICY
Acme Financial Services

1. Purpose
This policy establishes the framework for managing information security across
Acme Financial Services, ensuring the confidentiality, integrity, and
availability of company and customer information.

2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who
access Acme's information systems.

3. Information Security Objectives
Acme is committed to protecting its information assets from unauthorized
access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. Security objectives are
set annually by the IT department.

4. Organizational Structure
The IT Manager is responsible for day-to-day information security operations.
Security-related decisions are escalated to the IT Manager as needed.

5. Acceptable Use
Employees must use company systems only for business purposes. Personal use
of email and internet is permitted within reasonable limits.

6. Security Awareness
New employees receive a security overview during onboarding.

7. Incident Handling
Employees who notice unusual system behavior should contact the IT helpdesk.

8. Policy Enforcement
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and
including termination.

9. Policy Review
This policy is reviewed by the IT department on an as-needed basis.
